# CS Bank: things to try

No script. Install the app (QR code on the table), sign up, and try any of these. Two judges can do them together; one judge can pair with our team phone.

**1. Make a family (1 min).** Family → Show my family code. The other phone joins with the code and you confirm. Call each other whatever you like ("Mom", "Rohan").

**2. The family scam (2 min).** Ask someone to phone you (any real call). While on it, pay a new UPI ID, e.g. `quickhelp.relief@okaxis`, ₹50,000.
- CS Bank pauses and says why. Tap **Ask** next to your family member.
- Their phone rings: "Did you ask Mom for ₹50,000?" You see whether their phone is on a call at all.
- They press **No**: your payment stops, and the 1930 complaint is filled in.
- Or they don't answer ("my phone is broken"): send it to *their own* account instead, or send it with a one-hour hold you can still cancel.

**3. Call the bank as yourself (1 min).** Call CS Bank → Call as myself. Ask for your balance, or to change your registered mobile number. You'll be asked for your card digits (Profile) and approve on your phone.

**4. Try to break in (2 min).** Call CS Bank → Try an attack → pick your family member's number. Do your best impression, or play the cloned voice and type what it says. The app even shows you their "leaked" card digits. Watch the voice check, then watch the approval or code go to *their* phone, not yours.

**5. Something feels wrong?** Home → Block all payments. Then try paying anyone.

What to notice:
- Nothing risky ever rests on how a voice sounds.
- The bank's assistant tells you it's automated, and never reads codes or approvals from the caller's side.
- Family members never see each other's balance. Call status is shared only while they're checking with you, and only if they chose to share it.
